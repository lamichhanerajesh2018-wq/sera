## Interpretation

The strongest opportunity areas in this sample are:

1. Account-management reliability: deposits, withdrawals, account closures, debit-card access, and fees.
2. Debt ownership and validation: debts that consumers say are not theirs, wrong balances, identity-theft debts, and missing verification.
3. Credit-card dispute resolution: unresolved purchase disputes, unauthorized transactions, refunds, chargebacks, and statement errors.
4. Fraud and identity protection: unauthorized accounts or transactions and poor post-fraud resolution.
5. Payment and servicing operations: payment processing, payoff confirmation, interest, lien release, and inconsistent servicing communication.
